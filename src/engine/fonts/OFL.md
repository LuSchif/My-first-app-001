# Handwriting fonts

`cursive.json`, `lettering.json`, `print.json` and `casual.json` are converted from the SVG
single-line fonts EMS Allure, EMS Felix, EMS Readability and EMS Elfin (created by Sheldon B.
Michaels, SVG conversion by Windell H. Oskay, Evil Mad Scientist Laboratories), derivatives of
Allura (Rob Leuschke, TypeSETit), Felipa (Fontstage), Source Sans Pro Light (Paul D. Hunt,
Adobe) and Mountains of Christmas (Crystal Kluge, Tart Workshop). They were taken from the
`hersheytext` package (2.0.0) and reduced to the glyphs Broome uses, with curves flattened.
